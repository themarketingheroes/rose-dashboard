The headline

We took the account over on May 19. Across the three complete months since then, June through August, ad spend went from $7,691 to $17,722 and attributed sales went from $43,110 to $85,938, up 99%. Bookings rose 73%. ROAS moved from 5.6x to 4.9x, which is what happens when you scale spend into colder audiences, and that period still includes August, when ads were paused most of the month at your request.
